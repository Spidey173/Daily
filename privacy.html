Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

DailyDrop values your privacy. We collect minimal information necessary to process order deliveries, such as delivery addresses, phone numbers, and cart preferences stored locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Information

  • To fulfill and track your order status in real-time.
  • To provide customer support and delivery notifications.
  • To personalize your shopping experience across store categories.

3. Data Protection & Storage

Your local cart and order history are securely preserved in browser storage (`localStorage`). DailyDrop never sells, rents, or shares your personal details with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies & Local Data

We use essential client-side cookies and local storage tokens to remember your shopping cart items and order history across page refreshes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any privacy inquiries or request data removal, please email us at privacy@dailydrop.com.
